How Moneto Prediction works

Test USDT is play money for trying the product. Deposits and withdrawals here are simulations — no real funds move, and nothing has cash value.

Every market is a Yes/No question priced from live Polymarket data. A price of 62¢ means the market gives that outcome a ~62% chance. Each share pays 1 USDT if your outcome wins and 0 if it loses.

Example: Stake 100 USDT on Yes at 40¢ → you receive 250 shares. If Yes wins you get 250 USDT (profit +150). If No wins you lose the 100.

Cash out any time. You don't have to wait for the result. As the market moves in your favour, your shares are worth more — Portfolio shows the live cashout value and your profit. Sell all or part of a position; a 1% cashout fee applies. If a market is cancelled your remaining cost is refunded.

Orders fill at the live market price shown at confirmation (3% price-move protection). Markets above 98% / below 2% are closed for new positions. Results are taken from Polymarket when each market resolves.

Market data: Polymarket public APIs (read-only). Not financial advice.

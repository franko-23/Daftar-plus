Moneto Points are free play points. They are not your wallet balance and have no cash value.

You start with 1,000 points. Pick Yes or No on a market and stake 10–500 points.

If you are right you receive stake ÷ price. Example: 100 points on Yes at 40% returns 250 (profit +150). If you are wrong you lose the stake. If a market is cancelled, your stake is refunded.

One pick per market, no changes after confirming. Markets above 97% or below 3% are closed for picks. Results come from Polymarket once a market resolves.

Market data is live and read-only from Polymarket. Points are stored on this device only. Not financial advice.
